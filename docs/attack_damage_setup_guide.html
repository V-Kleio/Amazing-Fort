2Tune to taste (optional)

  • Difficulty curve — on the Attacker scene: Fire Interval Start/End (2.0 → 0.55s), Spread Start/End (10° → 45°), Initial Delay. Round length is Round Duration on the Attacking phase (15s).
  • Ball feel — on ball.tscn: bounce (0.55), max_speed (1400), hard_material_boost (1.08), collision_decay (0.12), and damage via damage_threshold (250) / damage_factor (0.15). Lower the factor / raise durability if furniture still breaks too fast.
  • Break SFX — assign Break Sfx on the furniture scenes (optional).
